Ever get the feeling that your apps are exponentially increasing?

Bring Them Together

Consolidate your story

Fictionfolder is made to integrate the functionality of all the tools necessary for creating stories. We hope to take the simple infrastructure it is right now and build it up to interact with all the formats and apps you already use.

The Project

It can be that fantasy novel you've always wanted to write, the triller you've been working on for a year, or the romance you just came up with. If it's a story idea that's going to need a lot of work, it's a project.

Create my first

Get started with a setting . . .

  • Search Wikipedia articles and add relevant paragraphs to the document.
  • Create research headers and in-text notes for ideas and references.

. . . Or a character

  • Fill out character attribute and development entry fields.
  • Search lists of character names and personalities.
  • Upload images/index and build your character's profile, from basic characteristics to advanced.

Turn it into a plot . . .

  • Create and develop narrative threads.
  • Use a thread tracker to make sure plot elements are properly opened and closed.
  • View project characters while plotting.

. . . Then a draft

  • Give narratives life in a simple, distraction-free environment.
  • Customive basic formatiing and view basic document data.
